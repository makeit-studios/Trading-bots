They illustrate the behavior we need to measure: a clean directional band may support continuation; arrows inside flat bands cluster; a new move can begin while the blue 200 EMA still points the other way; a long signal near a local top or a short signal near a local bottom can fail immediately. In particular, `Trend 1 .png` and `Trend 2.png` show silver arrows amid reversals and overlap, `Trend 4.png` shows gold M15 arrows in a declining market and a transition, and `trend 10.png` shows BTC changing direction before a slow EMA can establish a new trend. ## Revised hypothesis

At each **closed H4 bar**, classify each available Exness market independently. Only markets with a measurable directional move are eligible. On eligible markets, wait for a **closed M15 Simple Trender pullback arrow** pointing in the H4 direction. Place a stop entry beyond its signal candle, with fixed stop and target. The earlier rule requiring three M15 closes and then an M5 arrow is removed from this first comparison: those gates delayed entry, were not supported by measured evidence, and made it hard to identify which stage helped. M5 can be tested later as a separately frozen variant if M15 shows an edge.

The first test uses the following exact definitions. All indicators read completed bars only; the M15 signal is evaluated at the first tick of the next M15 candle. Broker bars, broker-server calendar days, and tick volume define the M15 VWAP approximation.

The Pine source comments also propose an **exit on the first close across EMA(low,26)**. The current first comparison deliberately holds the exit fixed at 2R so the H4 scanner can be isolated. A second predeclared experiment should compare that Pine exit with the same entry set and a protective stop. The first comparison does not establish which exit is better.

The Pine source distinguishes **initial trend entry** (a close on the new side of EMA(low,26), then a stop beyond that bar) from the plotted **pullback arrow** (a cross of smoothed VWAP while on the appropriate side of EMA(low,26)). The current test covers the arrow entry only. If its trade count remains too low, the next entry study should compare a precisely defined initial entry—first closed M15 bar on the H4 trend side of EMA(low,26) after a prior close on the other side—against the arrow entry, with the same H4 scan, risk, expiry, and exits. This would be a new experiment, not a retroactive explanation of the current results.

| Stage | Fixed rule |
| --- | --- |
| H4 direction | Compare the last closed H4 close with the close 12 H4 bars earlier. Sign of the change is direction. |
| H4 trend strength | `ER = abs(net close change) / sum(abs(each of 12 close-to-close changes))`; `moveATR = abs(net close change) / mean true range of last 14 closed H4 bars`. Require `ER >= 0.45`, `moveATR >= 1.5`, and the last two H4 closes on the directional side of EMA(low,26). A zero path or unavailable history is ineligible. |
| H4 score | `ER * min(moveATR, 4)`. This is recorded for diagnostics; the first per-symbol test uses eligibility and direction, not top-score ranking. |
| M15 arrow | Original Pine formula: long when the last closed M15 close is above EMA(low,26), its previous close is below the **current** EMA(VWAP,13), and the signal close is above that EMA(VWAP,13). Reverse for short. Require arrow direction = H4 direction in filtered mode. |
| Chase check | The directional distance from signal close to the farther band boundary, divided by M15 ATR14, must be `<= 0.50`. |
| Entry and stop | Buy stop one tick plus current spread above the signal high; sell stop one tick below the low. Stop one tick beyond the opposite candle extreme. Reject already crossed prices, invalid broker minimum distances, and spread / planned stop distance `> 0.10`. |
| Expiry and exit | Submit the pending entry with broker-side expiration after two subsequent M15 candles; reject symbols that do not allow specified expiration. Also cancel on H4 ineligibility or reversal. Initial target at 2 times entry-to-stop price distance. No trailing, break-even, or opposite-arrow exit in this first comparison. |
| Position size | `OrderCalcProfit` estimates the loss at the stop in account currency. Use 0.45% of current equity for that estimate (0.5% nominal with a 10% cost reserve), round volume **down** to broker step, and skip when minimum lot exceeds this budget. One position or pending entry per symbol. |

The 0.45, 1.5, 0.50, 0.10, 2R, and 0.5% numbers are **predeclared research controls**, not optimized values or conclusions from the screenshots. The 200 EMA is plotted by Pine but is not part of its arrow formula; it is not a hard filter here because it can lag a fresh move.

## First comparison and universe

`Experts/TrenderMarketResearchEA.mq5` is tester-only. Mode 0 trades every qualifying closed M15 arrow; mode 1 adds the H4 eligibility and directional match. Entry, stop, target, spread gate, position size, and expiry are identical between modes. It also logs H4 scans, signals, skips, orders, and cancellations. **Each test is one symbol at a time**. These runs show whether the H4 gate helps within a market; they do **not** show the performance of a combined scanner choosing among simultaneously eligible markets.

Audit correction: the first October batch used an EA that treated `OrderSend` retcode `DONE` as rejection even though the tester placed the pending order. Consequently it did not track that order for cancellation. The order-table audit found **51 fills after the intended expiry** across 20 runs, some hours or days late. Its results are retained as debugging evidence and **must not be used as strategy performance**. The corrected EA accepts successful `DONE` or `PLACED` retcodes, verifies the live order, and requires `ORDER_TIME_SPECIFIED` with the exact broker-side expiration. Only reports from a fresh rerun of that corrected binary count toward this comparison. [MQL5 symbol expiration modes](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/constants/environment_state/marketinfoconstants) and [order validity properties](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/constants/tradingconstants/orderproperties) document the mechanism.

The initial Exness universe is `XAUUSDm`, `XAGUSDm`, `EURUSDm`, `EURNZDm`, `NZDJPYm`, `US30m`, `US500m`, `USTECm`, `BTCUSDm`, `ETHUSDm`, subject to complete tester history and tradability. The additional FX crosses broaden the comparison beyond screenshots. `USTECm` is the NASDAQ proxy. SOL from the screenshots has no verified available Exness equivalent and is excluded. Any unavailable symbol must be reported as unavailable rather than silently omitted.

The user chose a four-market focus after the broad October screen: `XAUUSDm` (gold), `BTCUSDm` (crypto), `EURUSDm` (major FX), and `USTECm` (NASDAQ proxy). The ten-symbol July run was stopped at the user's request to save time and data after gold, silver, EURUSD, EURNZD, NZDJPY, and US30 had completed; its remaining partial run is not a ten-symbol comparison. Continue July only for BTC and USTEC, then run the focus four separately in August and September with the same frozen parameters. Gold and BTC are user-selected categories; EURUSD is the major FX pair with enough observed arrows to analyze, and USTEC directly matches the NASDAQ screenshots. This focus is for deeper measurement, not a declaration that these markets are profitable.

Test settings: Exness-MT5Trial9, USD 10,000 initial balance, leverage 1:2000, M15 Strategy Tester, `Model=4` real ticks requested, same 2026-10-01 through 2026-10-26 window for every market and both modes. Check each report's actual history-quality field. October screenshots and prior work already influenced this specification, so this window is diagnostic, **not a clean holdout**. July and other earlier windows can check regime sensitivity but have also been exposed to earlier strategy research. The cleanest future evaluation starts after the rules and software are frozen.

## Performance ledger and decisions

For each symbol and mode, save the tester `.set`, `.ini`, full HTML report, and parsed summary with actual history quality, total trades, net USD, profit factor, expected payoff per trade, equity drawdown, and long/short win rates. Count H4 eligible bars, aligned arrows, rejected arrows by reason, orders, expired orders, filled orders, and exit reasons from event logs. Reconstruct net realized R from planned account-currency stop risk and actual deal P&L. Audit spread, commissions, swaps, slippage, and any fallback/generated ticks before interpreting net results. These extra event and R fields still need a verified export pipeline; the HTML reports currently give the core realized metrics.

Pair suitability should be based on a consistent sample, minimum-lot feasibility, transaction costs relative to stop size, trade count, and performance stability across periods. Do not select only the October winners and call them validated. A three-trade result, even with a high profit factor, is inconclusive. Report no-trade markets as no evidence, not as zero-risk winners. Use uncertainty bands for net expectancy when sample size permits. Repeat a cost-stress test and evaluate months separately.

Only if the per-symbol evidence is promising should a second, synchronized **portfolio scanner** be built. At each H4 update it would rank eligible symbols by the fixed H4 score, cap simultaneous symbols (candidate: top three), cap total account risk including correlated markets, and resolve simultaneous signals deterministically. A portfolio backtest must run all symbols in one clock with broker-accurate fills, rather than add independent equity curves. No portfolio performance is claimed from the current EA.

The portfolio candidate rules are fixed **before** that build: rank by score descending at each closed H4 bar; break ties by symbol name; permit entries only among the top three eligible symbols; reserve no more than 0.5% planned equity risk per entry, 1.5% across all open and pending entries, and one entry at a time within each rough risk cluster (metals, US indices, crypto, FX). Process simultaneous M15 signals by score descending, then symbol name. A pending order consumes a risk slot until filled or cancelled. Do not close an existing position when its market drops out of the top three; its protective stop or target remains. Halt new entries for the broker-server day after strategy net equity falls 2% from that day's opening equity; this is an entry halt and cannot guarantee a 2% maximum loss. These are portfolio test rules, **not** behavior implemented by the current single-symbol EA.

## Research and operational basis

Long-horizon trend-following research supports testing directional persistence across markets, but does not establish an intraday Simple Trender edge ([Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen](https://fairmodel.econ.yale.edu/ec439/mosk.pdf)). MT5 can use real ticks where available and may substitute generated ticks when history is incomplete; report the actual tester quality for every run ([MQL5 tester documentation](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/runtime/testing)). Historic TradingView plots can differ from information available during a live candle, so the test uses closed arrows ([TradingView repainting documentation](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/repainting/)). Repeatedly choosing the best symbols or thresholds on the same sample risks selection bias ([Bailey et al.](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf)).
