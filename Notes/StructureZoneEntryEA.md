# StructureZoneEntryEA

Independent MT5 EA in `Experts/StructureZoneEntryEA.mq5`. It is designed for broker symbols beginning with `XAUUSD` and defaults to H4 main structure, M15 internal structure and zones, and M5 entry structure. Attach it to an XAUUSD M5 chart to see the detailed entry view. The three analysis timeframes and pivot sensitivities are inputs.

## Rules implemented

- Confirmed pivots use only bars available at confirmation time. A closed bar beyond a known swing wick is a structural break; a wick that returns inside is a sweep. Provisional rolling swing boundaries are dotted. The causal break never backdates an entry to the pivot candle.
- The last M15 candle before a closed directional departure is the base, regardless of body size. Its original full high and low are immutable box edges. A candidate appears after the close beyond its body. Imbalance, three-candle FVG, structure break, and sweep are independent evidence flags. Their on/off switches affect the minimum score, and no full-wick break is silently mandatory when its point is disabled.
- A zone breaks only on an M15 close beyond its far edge. Broken boxes end at the breaking candle and remain as gray outlines. The FVG point remains recorded after a fill.
- A visit starts on contact, including the live M15 bar. Taps remain one visit until a later M15 candle's wick reaches 60% of the path from the zone's near edge to the opposing M15 swing frozen at birth. That candle may still overlap the box; only a subsequent touching M15 candle starts the next visit.
- H4 sets the main direction; M15 can direct trades when H4 is unclear. M15 countertrend structure supports pullback entries. M5 closed structure breaks support confirmed entries. Simple Trender and high-impact USD news are displays, not independent entry vetoes.
- Main early limits use the zone near edge during an M15 pullback. Confirmed limits use the M5 break candle's complete high-low midpoint after M15 alignment. Pullback trades require M15 and M5 alignment. Both entry types can be used once per zone visit. Early TP is 50% of the frozen swing distance, confirmed TP is 90%, and initial SL is the original far edge. Pullback positions close on the selected opposing zone touch.
- Each first-visit entry sizes to 1% of equity; retests use 0.75% when H4 and M15 agree, otherwise 0.5%. Pending and open XAUUSD stop risks are included in the 4% cap. Volume is rounded down to the broker's step and never raised to minimum lot. Pending requests undergo price, stop, margin, `OrderCalcProfit`, `OrderCheck`, and server-result checks. Orders require a hedging account.

## Chart and audit

- The default `InpChartView=CHART_FOCUS` shows at most three relevant boxes: selected demand and supply first, then recent eligible zones or one recent candidate. Broken zones disappear from the chart in Focus but remain available in Detail. Selected boxes are filled; other boxes are outlines. Each box label names its M15 side, birth date/time, state, visit, and quality score; the panel and hover text add `I/F/B/S` evidence. `InpMaxDrawnZones` adjusts the count.
- Focus uses short recent line segments instead of full-width structure, visit, and order lines. Orange means H4 structure, blue M15, gold M5, white the frozen opposing swing, and yellow the 60% revisit marker. Aqua marks a pending entry, red its stop, and lime its target. Solid swing levels are confirmed; dotted M5 highs/lows are provisional; dashed M5 break levels require a closed candle. Hover any line for its full name and price.
- Pending order labels spell out BUY/SELL, EARLY/M5 MIDPOINT, visit number, and MAIN/PULLBACK/INTERNAL role. Duplicate stop and target prices share one line. Gray Simple Trender arrows are provisional; green/red arrows belong to closed candles.
- `InpChartView=CHART_DETAIL` restores all timeframe levels and FVG rectangles for the most recent `InpMaxDrawnZones` zones; increase that input to inspect more. Set `InpShowHistory=true` in Detail to inspect broken boxes. These are display settings only; hidden zones still participate in strategy rules.
- The chart panel shows the three structure directions, speed, USD news, Trender state, selected zone flags (`I/F/B/S`), pending count, reserved risk, and current decision. Timestamped state, order, cancel, fill, and exit events are written to `StructureZoneEntryEA_events.csv` in the terminal's MQL5 Files area (agent Files area during tests). Canceled pending orders now name the first failed condition, such as an M15 direction change, invalid zone, adverse M5 break, or target reached before fill.
- [Interactive decision walkthrough](../local_tester/reports/20261022_035322/szea_walkthrough.html) steps through a real August 17 tester example. It uses exact tester events and price levels, with no invented candlesticks. Its [journal excerpt](../local_tester/reports/20261022_035322/szea_walkthrough_log.txt) contains the source lines. The example includes an unfilled cancel, a confirmed fill, a second visit, and two stop losses.
- `InpVisualCaptureTime1` through `InpVisualCaptureTime3` can request chart PNGs at three broker-server timestamps in MT5 *visual* Strategy Tester. Zero disables each capture. The EA prints each screenshot result in the tester journal; MT5 writes the PNG under the terminal or testing agent's `MQL5/Files` directory. [run_szea_visual.ps1](../local_tester/run_szea_visual.ps1) configures a visual August 17 replay and refuses to start while another MT5 terminal session is open.
