This video serves as a comprehensive masterclass on **Market Structure**, which the instructor identifies as the most vital concept in technical analysis for maintaining consistent profitability.

### **Core Concepts of Market Structure (0:00 - 33:19)**

*   **Trend Basics:** Markets operate in three phases: **Uptrends** (higher highs and higher lows), **Downtrends** (lower lows and lower highs), and **Consolidations** (sideways movement). The instructor advises trading only in the direction of the trend (0:30).
*   **Impulses and Corrections:** Impulses are pro-trend moves that create new highs or lows, while corrections are counter-trend pullbacks that offer better entry prices for the next impulse (11:25).
*   **Mapping Structure:** Highs and lows are the raw indicators of trend direction. Mapping these accurately is essential for avoiding "falling knives" or trading against institutional order flow (05:28).
*   **Breaks of Structure (BoS):** A BoS occurs when price closes beyond a previous high or low, confirming the continuation of a trend or a reversal (22:55).

### **Common Traps and Advanced Strategies (33:19 - 1:08:59)**

*   **Internal Structure:** A frequent mistake is reacting to minor price moves within an impulse. The instructor emphasizes focusing on the "swing structure" to avoid being tricked by noise (33:19).
*   **Liquidity Sweeps:** These are false breakouts where price wicks beyond a level but fails to close, often leading to sharp reversals. Waiting for **candle body closures** is the key to differentiating a sweep from a valid BoS (38:55).
*   **Supply and Demand Trading:** To trade structure, identify imbalanced zones—the last candle before an impulsive move—to enter trades in the direction of the trend (41:55).
*   **Confirmation Entries:** Traders can lower risk by dropping to a **lower time frame** (e.g., 5-minute) once the price hits a supply or demand zone on the higher time frame to verify the structure shift (54:11).
*   **Swing vs. Sub-Structure:** Larger time frame "swing" trends contain smaller "sub-structure" trends. You can use this to take shorter-term trades in the direction of the sub-structure before re-joining the main swing trend (59:08).
